What’s in the grinder.

Turn on session scanning and Clausage breaks down what’s eating your Claude Code limit, with a tip for each: parallel sessions, long context, your top skills and subagents, and how many times you hit the wall this week.

It reads ~/.claude on your Mac. Only totals are kept, never prompts or code, and nothing leaves the Mac.

Nothing funny in the casing.

You sign in on claude.ai. Clausage reads the same usage page you’d check yourself, and nothing else.

What it readsYour plan’s type, limits, usage credits and how much you’ve used.
What it never seesYour password. You sign in on claude.ai’s own page, and Clausage never reads it.
Where it livesOn your device. The iPhone app is standalone, with no iCloud sync.

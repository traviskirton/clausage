Clausage support

Help with Clausage, the free usage tracker for Claude Pro and Max plans: how to reach us, and answers to the common questions.

Updated October 7, 2026 · Covers Clausage for Mac and the iPhone app (coming soon) · Independent, not made by Anthropic.

Something taste off? Tell us.

Bugs, ideas or numbers that look wrong all go to the developer. Say whether you’re on Mac or iPhone, and never send your password.

Which Claude plans does it work with?

Claude Pro and Max. Clausage reads your usage from claude.ai’s Settings → Usage page, and the Free plan doesn’t have one, so there’s nothing to count. On Free, Clausage says so instead of showing limits.

Why don’t my numbers match claude.ai?

Clausage shows the same numbers as Settings → Usage on claude.ai, read from your own account. When they differ, one of them is usually older, so refresh:

  • Mac: the popover shows when it last updated. Press ⌘R in the popover, or choose Refresh from the gear menu. Open claude.ai Usage, in the same menu, opens the page to compare.
  • iPhone app (coming soon): pull down on the Usage screen.

If claude.ai changes how its usage page works, Clausage may show an error instead of numbers until an update catches up. The Mac app updates itself. If you see this, please tell us.

How often do the iPhone widgets update?

This is for the iPhone app, which is coming soon. iOS decides when widgets update, usually every 15–30 minutes, while Refresh in background is on (Settings → Widgets; it’s on unless you turn it off).

To update right away, open the app, or tap Updated … ↻ at the top of the large widget.

How do I sign out or delete my data?

  • Mac: Settings → Accounts → Disconnect removes that account’s session, history and stored usage.
  • iPhone app: Settings → Sign Out. To remove everything, Settings → Privacy → Sign Out and Delete Everything also clears cached usage, weekly history and settings. Sign out before deleting the app, because Keychain entries can outlive it.

Clausage has no account or server of its own, so there’s nothing else to delete. Your Claude account is managed on claude.ai.

What does Clausage read, and where does it go?

Your plan, your limits and how much you’ve used, from claude.ai, using the session you signed in with. It never sees your password or your chats, and it keeps everything on your device: no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The privacy policy has the full list.

Is Clausage made by Anthropic?

No. Clausage is a free, open-source (MIT) app for Claude by an independent developer, and it isn’t made by or affiliated with Anthropic. For questions about your Claude account, plan or billing, go to Claude’s help center.

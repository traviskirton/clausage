October 7, 2026

Clausage 0.0.9

Updates now come out of the kitchen hot and ready, with no waiting at the counter.

  • Updates download quietly in the background, so they're plated before you notice. No more pop-up update windows.
  • When an update is ready, Settings opens once on Updates with Install Now front and center. A spinner keeps you posted while it installs.
  • Not hungry yet? Leave it on the pass, and the update installs itself when you quit Clausage.
  • The popover's Update available line and update notifications take you straight to the Updates pane.
  • See what's on the menu: the Updates pane shows the version you have and, when available, the release notes.
  • If you're already up to date, Check for Updates… sits right there in the same pane.
  • Settings got a fresh glaze, with a softer sidebar, crisper pane titles and warmer button colors.
October 7, 2026

Clausage 0.0.8

A fresh garnish for the menu bar: your usage can now show up in your own colors.

  • Turn on Fill in accent color in Settings and Fill mode tops its rays with your system accent color. It's off by default, so the plain monochrome look stays put.
  • Change your system accent color and the menu bar icon switches to match right away. No reheating needed.
  • Rays now fill clockwise from the top, like a plate served in order. Accent-colored rays sit on solid white ones, so they're easier to read.
October 7, 2026

Clausage 0.0.7

Limit alerts now ring the dinner bell before your plate's full, not after.

  • New installs warn you at 85% and 95% by default, the same points where the bars turn orange and red.
  • Under Notifications, "Any limit reaches" now offers 50%, 75%, 85% and 95% chips. Pick the servings you want flagged.
  • Thresholds you already turned on stay on, so your usual helpings are kept.
October 7, 2026

Clausage 0.0.6

A fresh plate for the popover and Settings, with your usage served up front.

  • The popover has a new look: a cleaner title row, a quieter plan tag, bigger percentages and rounded bars. Same numbers, nicer plating.
  • Settings now opens on a new Usage page with your limits, the running-total chart, your product split and usage credits, all on one plate.
  • Usage credits now show on your Mac too and refresh every hour, so you know what's left in the pantry.
  • The Settings sidebar is reordered, with a fresh icon on each page. Switches, menus, buttons and the shortcut recorder now share the warm Ember color.
  • Settings no longer opens with a focus ring on the sidebar. Press Tab and it comes back.
  • The scroll bar in Settings now sits in the margin instead of on top of your boxes.
October 6, 2026

Clausage 0.0.5

Free plans get a proper table setting, Google sign-in stops stalling, and Ventura pulls up a chair.

  • Sign in with Google now goes through instead of stalling on a blank page. Google's popup opens in its own window and hands you back to claude.ai.
  • On a Free plan, Clausage now shows an empty tally and "Nothing on the plate yet", with the reason, instead of empty bars or "Couldn't update".
  • The menu bar icon stays empty on a Free plan, in every display mode. From the popover, Use another account switches you to a Pro or Max account.
  • Limit alerts pause while you're on a Free plan, but Clausage keeps your choices. It checks your plan on every refresh, so the bars come back as soon as you upgrade.
  • Clausage now runs on macOS 13 Ventura. There it holds one account at a time, and Use another account swaps in a new one.
October 6, 2026

Clausage 0.0.4

Claude Usage has a new name on the menu: say hello to Clausage.

  • Same recipe, new name and a fresh icon. Updates keep arriving as usual, but you'll need to sign in once more after this one.
  • Settings has a new tablecloth: every selected switch, chip and segment is Ember, dark mode runs warmer, and the window has proper macOS corners.
  • About now tells you when an update is cooking, with Install right there. It also has Send Feedback and links to the website, privacy policy and acknowledgements.
  • Notify me when has moved to the Notifications pane, so all your alerts come from one kitchen.
  • Fewer side dishes in General: options only show up when they apply, and Refresh now sits as a note under Refresh every.
  • The Ring menu bar style is off the menu, so if you used it you're now on Bars. Fill now keeps a White or Dark base when you pin one.

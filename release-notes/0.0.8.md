A fresh garnish for the menu bar: your usage can now show up in your own colors.

- Turn on **Fill in accent color** in Settings and Fill mode tops its rays with your system accent color. It's off by default, so the plain monochrome look stays put.
- Change your system accent color and the menu bar icon switches to match right away. No reheating needed.
- Rays now fill clockwise from the top, like a plate served in order. Accent-colored rays sit on solid white ones, so they're easier to read.

The menu bar sticks to the main course now, and the weekly chart gets a cleaner plating.

- The menu bar icon always shows All models now, instead of jumping to whichever limit is fullest. Tint percent by pressure follows All models too, so you watch one plate.
- If your plan has no All models limit, the icon shows Session instead, so it's never empty.
- The weekly chart marks each reading at the hour it happened. If your week resets early, the line drops straight down like a plate cleared ahead of schedule.
- The even-pace line now shows a true steady pace, so you can see at a glance whether you're eating ahead of schedule.
- The chart plots a reading every six hours on a slimmer line, so it reads as a steady climb instead of a jittery one. Last week's line is thinner too and sits further in the background.
- New in Settings: Keep history saves older weeks for 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or Forever. It's still 2 weeks by default, and the history stays on your Mac.
